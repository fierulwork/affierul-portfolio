      Case study · Focus-area capacity planner

      The station view was not enough.

      I needed to make daily capacity decisions for selected areas. Existing reporting operated at station level, and no ready-to-use tool gave me the local view I needed. So I built decision support around the problem.

      The leadership decisionDefine the focus area, challenge the workload assumptions, and test deployment options before committing resources.

      The tool’s roleConnect mapping, demand and operating constraints. Support judgement with scenarios and feasibility checks.

      Interactive reconstructionSynthetic data · fictional areas
      Station overview

      Schematic map. All locations, workload units and capacity values in this demonstration are invented.

      01 / Diagnose

      An aggregate can hide a local constraint.

      The station total fits within available capacity. That does not mean every area has enough coverage. Inspect the local distribution before deciding.

      Station demand / capacity290 / 300 units
      Area A demand / capacity140 / 100 units

      Toy model: capacity is transferable between adjacent areas. Total capacity stays fixed. Real deployment also requires route, time-window, skill and service feasibility checks.
